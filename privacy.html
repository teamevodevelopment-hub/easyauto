1. Who is responsible

EasyAuto is a team within Trucktown Ontario Inc. ("Trucktown", "we", "us" or "our"). Trucktown is responsible for the personal information collected through this website and in connection with your financing application. When we submit your application to lenders, we do so in Trucktown's name as the dealer, on your behalf.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws. Our Privacy Officer is responsible for our compliance with this policy (see Contact).

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: full name, email address, phone number.
  • Address: street address, unit, city, province, postal code, and how long you have lived there.
  • Personal details: date of birth, and whether you rent or own your home and your monthly housing cost.
  • Employment and income: source of income, employer or business name, job title, how long you have had this income, and monthly income before taxes.
  • Your preferences: your monthly payment budget and the type of vehicle you want.
  • Your consent: a record that you accepted our Terms of Use and this policy and authorized a credit check, with the date and time.

Information from others

  • Credit information from consumer reporting agencies such as Equifax Canada and TransUnion Canada, including your credit report and score.
  • Lender decisions, such as whether a lender approved your application and the terms offered.

Information collected automatically

  • The page address you applied from, and advertising campaign details in that address (for example utm_source, gclid or fbclid), so we know which ad or website brought you to us.
  • Technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, collected in our hosting provider's server logs.

3. How we use it

  • To review your application and submit it to lenders so they can assess your request for financing.
  • To obtain your credit report and help match you with suitable lenders and vehicles.
  • To contact you by phone, text and email about your application, financing options and vehicle.
  • To complete your vehicle purchase and arrange delivery anywhere in Canada.
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud.
  • To measure how well our advertising works and improve our website.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Lenders in our network (over 40 banks, finance companies and other lenders across Canada), to whom Trucktown submits your application so they can assess and, if approved, provide financing.
  • Consumer reporting agencies, to obtain your credit report and as lenders report credit activity.
  • Service providers who work on our behalf and must protect your information, such as our website host (Vercel), Google (address suggestions as you type your address), our customer management and email/text messaging providers, and vehicle transport companies that deliver your vehicle.
  • Advertising platforms such as Google and Meta, which may receive limited information (for example, that someone who clicked an ad submitted an application) to measure ad performance. We do not share your application details with them.
  • Others when required or permitted by law, for example to respond to a court order, prevent fraud, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business, where the recipient agrees to protect your information.

5. Your consent

When you submit an application and check the consent box, you consent to us collecting, using and sharing your personal information as described in this policy, including obtaining your credit report. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Privacy Officer, subject to legal or contractual restrictions. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer process your application. A lender that has already received your application handles your information under its own privacy policy.

You can stop marketing messages at any time by replying STOP to a text, using the unsubscribe link in an email, or contacting us.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website uses your browser's storage to make the application form work, and it loads Google Maps to suggest addresses as you type. What you type in the address field is sent to Google to provide those suggestions. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. If we use advertising tools such as the Google tag or Meta Pixel, they may set cookies to measure ad performance. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

7. Storage outside Canada

Some of our service providers, including our website host and Google, may store or process personal information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it is outside Canada, it is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessed by its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities. We require our service providers to protect your information.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal, tax, regulatory and dealer record-keeping requirements. After that, we securely delete or anonymize it. If your application does not proceed, we keep it only as long as reasonably needed to respond to questions, prevent fraud and meet those requirements.

9. How we protect it

We use physical, organizational and technical safeguards suited to the sensitivity of the information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) when you submit your application, access limited to staff who need it, and contractual protections with service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a privacy breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the appropriate privacy regulator as required by law.

10. Your rights

You may ask to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and learn how it has been used and shared;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete; and
  • withdraw your consent, as described above.

To make a request, contact our Privacy Officer. We will respond within the time required by law, usually 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the privacy regulator in your province (for example in Quebec, Alberta or British Columbia).

11. Quebec residents

If you live in Quebec, you also have rights under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. These include the right to have your information de-indexed or its dissemination stopped in certain cases, and the right to receive your computerized personal information in a structured, commonly used technological format. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec. Our Privacy Officer, who can be reached at the address below, is the person in charge of the protection of personal information.

12. Minors

Our services are intended for people who have reached the age of majority in their province or territory. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version takes effect when it is posted on this page with a new effective date. If we make significant changes to how we use your information, we will ask for your consent where required.
